About Me

I'm a graphic designer and illustrator. I enjoy creating visual identities, illustrations, and graphic pieces that communicate clearly, creatively, and authentically. I always aim for every project to make sense, tell a story, and connect with the audience.

I work with Adobe Suite, Procreate, and Figma, tools that allow me to bring each project to life in a versatile and professional manner.
